Privacy Policy for Snap Pazzel

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Snap Pazzel ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Snap Pazzel mobile game, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Snap Pazzel may collect or store:

  • Player name or display name
  • Player ID and an anonymous Firebase identifier
  • Game scores, best times, puzzle progress, and related gameplay statistics
  • Leaderboard information when you use leaderboard features
  • Technical/device information that may be processed by advertising services

We do not ask for passwords, bank account details, or payment card information in the game.

2. How We Use Information

  • Save and restore your game progress
  • Maintain leaderboards and gameplay statistics
  • Provide and improve game features and performance
  • Identify and prevent abuse or cheating
  • Display advertisements
  • Respond to support requests

3. Firebase Services

Snap Pazzel uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support anonymous player identification and securely store gameplay-related information such as player data, scores, progress, and leaderboard information.

4. Advertising

Snap Pazzel uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain device or advertising-related information in accordance with Google's privacy practices and applicable laws. Advertising identifiers and related data may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

5. Camera, Gallery, Internet, and Device Features

The app may request access to device features such as the internet connection, camera or gallery, vibration, and audio when required by specific game features. These permissions are used only for the relevant functionality.

6. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used by the app, such as Firebase and Google AdMob, to provide the services described in this policy.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures and the security features provided by our service providers to protect stored information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Snap Pazzel is intended for general users. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information. If you believe that information has been provided by a child inappropriately, please contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

9. Your Choices

You may choose not to use optional features such as leaderboards or features that require additional device permissions. You can also manage advertising preferences through your device and Google settings where available.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
